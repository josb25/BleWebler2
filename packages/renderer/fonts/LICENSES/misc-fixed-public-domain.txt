X11 "misc-fixed" bitmap fonts (5x7, 5x8, 6x9, 6x10, 6x12, 6x13, 6x13B, 7x13,
7x13B, 7x14, 7x14B, 8x13, 8x13B, 9x15, 9x15B, 10x20)

These fonts are in the PUBLIC DOMAIN.

They originate from the X Window System core bitmap fonts and were extended to
Unicode/ISO 10646 by Markus Kuhn and contributors as part of the "ucs-fonts"
package. From that project's documentation:

    "The X11 misc-fixed fonts are in the public domain."

Source: https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/~mgk25/ucs-fonts.html
