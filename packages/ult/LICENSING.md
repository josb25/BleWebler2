# Licensing and provenance

ULT uses a deliberately simple licensing model:

- specification text and repository documentation are licensed under MIT;
- example template documents are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0, as declared in each file; and
- implementations are separate works and may choose their own compatible licences.

Contributors must submit original work or material whose licence permits inclusion and redistribution. Every commit must include a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off as described in the monorepo [contribution guide](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). Do not contribute proprietary schemas, copied implementation details, or material with unclear provenance.

The repository does not grant rights to third-party names, logos, fonts, product designs, or other trademarks and assets that a template might reference. Template authors and distributors remain responsible for those rights.
