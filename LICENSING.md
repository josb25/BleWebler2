# Licensing

BleWebler2's original code is available under the [MIT License](LICENSE). Bundled software, icons and fonts retain their own licences and are listed in [Third-party notices](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

The [ULT specification](packages/ult) is MIT-licensed. Its example templates are released under CC0 1.0 as declared in each document.

## Contributions

Contributions must be original or available under terms that permit inclusion in this repository. Every commit must include the Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off described in [Contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).

Protocol contributions should record their sources and include focused tests. Do not commit proprietary applications, firmware, restricted captures, credentials or third-party code without compatible terms and complete notices.

## Third-party material

When adding or adapting third-party material, include its name, version or revision, source, licence, required copyright notices and any required statement of modifications. Keep required licence texts in the repository and update [Third-party notices](THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md).

Production dependencies are locked in `package-lock.json`. Release review includes dependency auditing, licence review, type-checking, tests and builds.
